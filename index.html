About the Platform

SM – Darul Ifta is a research tool that searches fatwas issued by various established Darul Iftas, helping users locate scholarly answers to their religious questions in one place.

Disclaimer

The fatwas made available through this application are presented for scholarly benefit and general religious guidance. A fatwa shown here is not necessarily the final or binding religious ruling for every individual or situation, and the presence of any fatwa in this app does not necessarily reflect the personal agreement or endorsement of this project or its administrator.

Fatwas from different Darul Iftas are presented as their own independent scholarly output. Each fatwa should therefore be understood in the context of its original source and the issuing Darul Ifta, and users are encouraged to consult a qualified Mufti for guidance on their specific situation.
