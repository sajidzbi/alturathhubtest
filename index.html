About This Platform

Al-Turath NotebookLMs Hub is a dedicated digital platform for Islamic academic research, bringing together classical Islamic scholarship and modern AI technology in one place. It hosts a growing collection of works in Arabic and Urdu, covering a wide range of Islamic sciences, all organised within multiple specialised Notebook LM's Collections.


The platform features an advanced search system and powerful research tools, making it easy to locate, explore, and study scholarly works across categories and time periods. It is designed to serve teachers, researchers, students, and academic institutions who seek a reliable and efficient research environment.


The library currently includes books from Maktabah Shamela, along with hundreds of additional titles from other sources. Further books will be added on a continuing basis, insha'Allah.
